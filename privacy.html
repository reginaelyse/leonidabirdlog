LEONIDA BIRD LOG

PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 19 November 2026

The short version: when you create a profile by signing in with Google, we store your email address, a display name and your bird sightings so your progress is saved to your account and follows you between devices. We never sell your data, show ads or use tracking cookies, and you can delete your account at any time.

Who we are

Leonida Bird Log (leonidabirdlog.com) is a free, unofficial fan-made birdwatching checklist for the game Grand Theft Auto VI. It is run by an individual in the United Kingdom, who is the data controller for the personal data described here. It is not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

Contact: reginaelyse@gmail.com

What we collect when you create a profile

Profiles are created by signing in with your Google account. When you do, we receive and store:

DataWhy we need it
Your Google account ID and email addressTo recognise your account each time you sign in, and to contact you about your account if we ever need to.
A display nameTo label your profile. You can change it at any time.
Your bird sightings (species, counts and when you logged them)To save your checklist and sync it across your devices.

We only ask Google for your basic profile and email address. We can't see your Google password, contacts, emails, files or anything else in your Google account.

The first time you sign in, any progress already saved in your browser is copied into your profile so you don't lose it.

Our use of information received from Google follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We use it only to provide the sign-in and checklist features described here. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell or transfer it to anyone else.

Using the site without a profile

You can still use the checklist without signing in. Your ticks and counts are then saved only in your browser on your own device and are never sent to us. Share card images are always created on your device and only leave it if you choose to save or post them.

Our legal basis (UK GDPR)

  • Providing the service you asked for: storing your account and sightings when you sign in.
  • Legitimate interests: keeping the site secure and running, for example basic server logs kept by our hosting providers.

Services we use

These companies process data on our behalf, only to run the site:

  • Supabase: stores profiles and sightings, in the United States (East US).
  • Google: handles "Sign in with Google". Google Fonts also supplies the site's fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers when you visit.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log technical details such as your IP address for security.

Some of these providers store data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where that happens, the transfer is covered by safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or Standard Contractual Clauses in their terms.

Cookies and tracking

We don't use advertising or analytics cookies, and we don't track you across other websites. The site uses your browser's local storage to keep you signed in and to save your checklist. These are strictly necessary for the site to work.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and sightings until you delete your account. When you do, they are permanently removed from our database within 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, download or delete the personal data we hold about you, or object to how we use it. You can change your display name and delete your account yourself from your profile on the site, or email us at the address above and we'll respond within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Age

You must be 13 or older to create a profile. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has signed up, contact us and we'll delete the account.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For significant changes affecting profiles, we'll let you know on the site.
