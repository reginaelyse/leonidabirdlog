About Leonida Bird Log

Leonida Bird Log is a free, unofficial birdwatching checklist for players of Grand Theft Auto VI. The game's state of Leonida is home to around 62 bird species, from flamingos and pelicans in the Keys to ospreys over the Grassrivers swamps. Leonida Bird Log helps you keep track of which ones you've found.

What you can do

  • Tick off each bird species as you spot it in the game, grouped by habitat: wetlands, coast, skies, woods and city.
  • Count how many times you've seen each bird, and track your progress towards all 62.
  • See which birds are confirmed in the game, marked with a verified badge.
  • Add birds that aren't on the list yet, and remove ones that turn out not to be in the game.
  • Make a share card image of your progress to post on social media.

Do I need an account?

No. Everyone can use the full checklist without signing in, and your progress is saved in your browser. If you'd like your checklist on more than one device, you can optionally sign in with Google. Leonida Bird Log then uses your Google account's name and email address only to create your profile and save your bird sightings to it. We never post anything to your Google account, access any other Google data, or share your information with anyone. You can delete your profile and all its data at any time.
